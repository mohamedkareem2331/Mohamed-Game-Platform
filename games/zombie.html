📖 Game Overview & Mechanics

Engineered by Mohamed Kareem, NIGHTFALL is a high-intensity 360-degree top-down survival arcade game. The browser edition runs the authentic game engine directly on HTML5 Canvas, guaranteeing 100% preservation of physics, enemy speeds, collision logic, particles, and rogue-lite upgrades.

  • Relentless Undead Waves: Escalating horde counts with varied health pools and rush speeds.
  • Boss Battles: Massive, high-health boss encounters spawn every 5th wave.
  • Rogue-lite Upgrades: Level up through XP crystals to choose from 3 randomized perks (Fire Rate, Bullet Damage, Speed, Dash Cooldown, Max Health).
  • Kinetic Combat: Includes tactical dash with iframe window, particle splatters, screen shake, and floating damage numbers.

🎮 Controls & Keyboard Mapping
