📖 Game Overview & Technical Mechanics

Created by Mohamed Kareem, this arcade retro-shooter incorporates original custom pixel starship and alien sprites, layered parallax nebulae, and synthesized 8-bit sound effects.

  • Energy Ammo Management: 50-shot magazine capacity with real-time 5 shots/sec auto-recharging when firing pauses.
  • 4-Tier Weapon Evolution: Collect green pickups to evolve lasers: Single → Dual → Triple → Quadruple plasma beams!
  • 7 Difficulty Modes: Very Easy, Easy, Normal, Hard, Expert, Insane, Ultra Extreme with distinct formations and boss health scaling.
