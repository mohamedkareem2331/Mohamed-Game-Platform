📖 Game Overview & Dungeon Systems

Created for Mohamed Kareem, Hollow Keep is a dark fantasy dungeon maze crawler. Navigate procedural labyrinth keeps against the clock, defeat mythical beasts in rhythmic sword and shield duels, upgrade your loadout in the Armory, fill your Bestiary, unlock legendary relics, and conquer Lord Hollow himself!

  • Sword & Shield Combat: Read enemy telegraphs! Strike with Attack when the ring glows, and raise Shield against enemy bursts.
  • Procedural Labyrinth Keeps: Level 1 grants 3 minutes, with escalating dungeon sizes, rooms, and timers for subsequent levels.
  • Armory Upgrades: Equip unlocked Weapons (Iron Blade, Broadsword, Flame Saber), Helmets, and Shoes with unique stat perks.
  • Bestiary & Relic Shelf: Discover 11 unique monster species (Wisps, Imps, Bats, Slimes, Ghouls, Spiders, Gargoyles, Wraiths, Knights, Hounds, Boss) and ancient relics.
  • Endless Mode & Trophies: Push your survival records in the Endless Run and collect prestigious mastery trophies.
  • Hearth Cat Companion: Visit Mossy the hearth cat in the tavern between expeditions for good fortune!
