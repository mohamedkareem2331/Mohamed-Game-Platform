📖 Game Overview & Expedition Mechanics

Created by Mohamed Kareem, Dungeon: The Hollow Crown is a tactical turn-based dungeon crawler. Descend through 5 procedurally generated floors of ancient ruins, manage hunger and wounds, collect enchanted relics, and conquer the final Guardian to claim the Hollow Crown.

  • Turn-Based Tactical System: Enemies only react and move when you take an action. Plan every step carefully!
  • Dynamic Fog of War & Minimap: Explore unmapped chambers with living line-of-sight and an auto-revealing minimap.
  • 5 Handcrafted Procedural Floors: The Forgotten Gate, Mossbound Halls, The Sunken Keep, Ember Vault, and The Hollow Throne.
  • Loot & Upgrades: Chests automatically enhance weapon tiers and armor durability.
  • Resource Management: Monitor Hunger (eat rations with F) and restore Health with potions (Q).
  • Automatic LocalStorage Saves: Progress saves automatically in your browser so you can continue your expedition anytime.
